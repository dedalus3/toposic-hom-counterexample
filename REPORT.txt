MEANING AND PROVED CONNECTIONS OF THE DEFINITIONS

EtaleTopos X is Sheaf X.smallEtaleTopology (Type u), not a replacement action
category. GeometricMorphism stores an actual adjunction and preservation of
finite limits by its inverse image. The structural morphism is constructed in
MultiplicativeGroup.lean and EtaleMorphisms.lean using the small affine etale
subsite; the arithmetic structural geometric morphism is its actual composite
with affineBaseEtaleMorphism (primeBase p).

RelativeMorphism pi rho stores a comparison f_* >>> rho_* isomorphic to pi_*.
RelativeCompatible f h gamma is precisely, for every sheaf M,
  rho.map (gamma.hom.app M) >> h.comparison.hom.app M = f.comparison.hom.app M.
RelativeEndomorphismClass is the quotient of these actual relative geometric
morphisms by existence of such a compatible natural isomorphism. Thus unequal
classes mean exactly no relative 2-isomorphism; no weaker equivalence relation
on point actions is used.

FiniteTypeSchemes S is the actual finite-type over-category of Scheme.{u}.
Universal homeomorphism is defined by every scheme base change being a
homeomorphism of topological spaces. Q is mathlib's Gabriel-Zisman localization.
There is no restriction on zigzags, intermediate schemes, reducedness, or
mixed characteristic. The monoid comparisons in the final statements are
constructed from actual etale direct images of exhaustive UH roofs.

The following bridges are proved, not assumed:
* localizedScheme_morphism_has_roof: every localized arrow has an actual
  finite-type UH roof. UH sources above G_m are proved affine.
* multiplicativeGroupUH_etaleDirectImage_isEquivalence: every denominator
  in these roofs induces an equivalence of the actual etale sheaf categories.
* localizedRoofs_common_refinement: equal localized arrows have a common
  actual finite-type UH refinement. The proof develops the right calculus of
  fractions, using the surjective closed diagonal of a UH to equalize maps.
* etaleDirectImage_composition_coherence: comparison coherence is proved as
  equality of actual iterated-pullback maps on the sites.
* localizedEtaleRealization_unique and localizedEtaleEndomorphism_independent:
  all roof realizations of one arrow are relatively isomorphic.
* localizedEtaleEndomorphism_comp, localizedEtaleEndomorphism_id,
  localizedEtaleEndomorphism_map: realization respects composition, identity,
  and ordinary scheme direct images, with their structural comparisons.
* localizedEtaleEndomorphismHom packages these laws as a monoid homomorphism
  from ALL localized endomorphisms to actual relative-isomorphism classes.
* primeFieldFiniteTypeHomEquiv and primeFieldEndomorphismEquiv identify the
  genuine arithmetic Hom set with the finite-field one. The adjunction proof
  covers arbitrary localized zigzags, including mixed-characteristic objects.
* arithmeticRoofAsFiniteFieldRoof and arithmeticEtaleEndomorphismHom_roof:
  EVERY arithmetic roof above G_m/F_p agrees with the arithmetic realization.
  Its source has an actual F_p-structure via its denominator. No restriction
  to initially characteristic-p zigzags is made.
* relativeAffineEtaleMorphism_postcompose checks the structural comparison
  when passing from f over k to f over the composite base. Thus postcomposition
  is connected to the actual scheme-induced etale morphism over Spec Z.

This is the sufficient localized endomorphism comparison. A functor on all
localized schemes and a scheme-perfection equivalence are not asserted.

ALGEBRAIC OBSTRUCTION AND THE SHORTER UH BRIDGE

RadicialPowers.lean, AffineUHPowers.lean, and LaurentUH.lean prove power descent
over the normal Laurent base, including nonreduced UH sources.
LocalizedMonomial.lean proves the monomial POINT ACTION for ALL localized
endomorphisms, using arbitrary roofs. Negative exponents, an infinite set of

RoofNonalgebraicity.lean then proves that a genuine relative roof triangle

ARITHMETIC ARGUMENT

The arithmetic twist is exactly the finite-field twist with its structural
comparison postcomposed by the actual i_* for Spec F_p -> Spec Z.
RelativeBaseChange.lean proves preservation of relative triangles and relative
invertibility under this operation.

The proof uses a narrower direct separation argument instead of assuming the
closed-subtopos full-faithfulness theorem. For each algebraic z in characteristic
p choose n > 0 with z^(p^n) = z. ArithmeticEtaleCoordinates.lean constructs the
actual standard etale Z-algebra for X^(p^n)-X with its derivative inverted. Its
derivative at z is -1, including p=2. The lift and its section value are proved.
ArithmeticBaseChange.lean checks the actual structural-comparison triangle;
arithmetic_postcompose_point_eq then forces equal coordinates for arithmetic
relative point isomorphisms. ArithmeticRoofNonalgebraicity.lean repeats the
actual roof obstruction using this proved separation. Together with the
arbitrary-zigzag arithmetic localization comparison, this proves the second
final theorem without any unproved full-faithfulness hypothesis.
